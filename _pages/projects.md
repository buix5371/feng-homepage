<section markdown="1" class="project"><p class="label">01 / Writing and generation</p><h2>Writing Assistance and Controllable Generation</h2>

Writing systems must organize information and adapt their output to a task. My work examines generation from structured inputs, summarization, and controllable text generation. Applications described in our research include essay generation, document writing, meeting summaries, and length-controlled summarization. TableGPT provides a research example of generating descriptions from tables with limited training examples.

[Read the TableGPT paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.coling-main.179/)
</section>

<section markdown="1" class="project"><p class="label">02 / Grounded interaction</p><h2>Knowledge-Enhanced Dialogue</h2>

Useful dialogue systems need to draw on relevant knowledge while remaining consistent with the evidence they receive. This research direction connects knowledge-enhanced dialogue generation with retrieval-augmented modeling, noise filtering, and contextual faithfulness. Industry collaborations described in the supplied research materials include a Huawei project on knowledge-enhanced dialogue generation. Related publications examine how retrieved evidence can be filtered and how model behavior can be aligned with context.

[Retrieval noise filtering](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.59/) · [Contextual faithfulness](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.826/)
</section>

<section markdown="1" class="project"><p class="label">03 / Multilingual communication</p><h2>Multilingual Machine Translation</h2>

My research also addresses multilingual machine translation and continual learning, with the aim of supporting communication across languages. Projects include research on continual learning for multilingual neural machine translation and collaboration on multilingual translation platforms. These efforts connect representation learning with practical translation systems. Work with Peng Cheng Laboratory is described in the supplied project materials; the research group website provides further context on collaborations.

[Visit the research group](https://ir.hit.edu.cn/19606/list.htm) · [Browse related publications](https://aclanthology.org/people/xiaocheng-feng/)
</section>
