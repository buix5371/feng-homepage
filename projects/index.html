Selected Application

Gaokao Essay Generation

Our group developed an end-to-end system that generates argumentative essays for China's national college entrance examination (Gaokao). The work was supported by successive national research programs and underwent third-party evaluation in 2022. Related technologies support document drafting, proofreading, and meeting summarization.
