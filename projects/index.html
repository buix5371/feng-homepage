Selected Applications

Gaokao Essay Generation

Our group developed an end-to-end system that generates argumentative essays for China's national college entrance examination (Gaokao). The work was supported by successive national research programs and underwent third-party evaluation in 2022. Related technologies support document drafting, proofreading, and meeting summarization.

Multilingual Machine Translation and Information Generation

Collaboration with Peng Cheng Laboratory contributes to the multilingual Naohai model and translation platforms. Work with Qiyuan Laboratory supports the Jiuge model and multilingual information generation.

Translation models have also been deployed on platforms associated with China International Communications Group and the Ministry of Education's national language-resource services.
