Xiaocheng Feng

Professor · School of Artificial Intelligence

Natural language processing & trustworthy text generation

I am a Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at Harbin Institute of Technology, where I lead the Trustworthy Generation (TG) group. My research focuses on natural language processing, particularly text generation and trustworthy language models.

I study how models represent diverse inputs, incorporate knowledge, and generate text that is factually grounded and consistent with its context. My work spans representation learning, knowledge-enhanced modeling, retrieval-augmented generation, and memory mechanisms for language models, with applications in writing assistance and multilingual machine translation.

I also contribute to the research community through conference reviewing, professional societies, and academic events.

Our work has received the ACL 2026 Best Resource Paper Award and the CCL 2021 Best English Paper Award.
