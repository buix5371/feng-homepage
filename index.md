I am a Professor at Harbin Institute of Technology, where I lead the Text Generation group. My research focuses on natural language processing, particularly text generation and trustworthy language models.

I study how models represent diverse inputs, incorporate knowledge, and generate text that is factually grounded and consistent with its context. My work spans representation learning, knowledge-enhanced modeling, retrieval-augmented generation, and memory mechanisms for language models, with applications in writing assistance and multilingual machine translation.

Selected work includes CodeBERT, TableGPT, and a survey of hallucination in large language models. I also contribute to the research community through conference reviewing, professional societies, and academic events.
